How to Play

  • Goal: destroy every rival Keep. Lose yours and you are out.
  • Gold: select Workers and right-click a Gold Mine. They carry gold back to your nearest Keep. Farms also trickle gold.
  • Mana: a Mana Well slowly fills your mana. Casters, healers and Elites cost mana to train.
  • Building: select a Worker, pick a building from the command card (or its hotkey) and click the ground. Workers build it.
  • Heroes: the Barracks trains your faction's heroes. The Hero Shrine trains Elites (one of each at a time). Squads come out as 3 units.
  • Army slots: heroes take 1 to 5 slots by price, as in Hollow Vale. Cottages and the Hero Shrine raise the cap. A squad gives slots back as members fall (whole slots for survivors, rounded down). Workers don't use slots.
  • Commands: left-click or drag to select. Right-click to move, attack or mine. A then click: attack-move. S: stop. H: hold position. Ctrl+1-9 saves a group, 1-9 recalls it. Space selects your army, . (period) an idle worker, Tab cycles your Keeps.
  • Faction powers from Hollow Vale work in real time: Formation, Bloodthirst, Undying, Burn, Regrowth, Siegecraft and Arcane Flow. Heroes keep their keywords: Armor, Splash, Stun, Drain, Heal, Siege, Flying, Charge and Squad. Kills earn XP; at 2 and 5 XP a hero earns a star.
  • Camera: arrow keys, push the mouse to the screen edge, or drag the minimap. Mouse wheel zooms. P pauses.
