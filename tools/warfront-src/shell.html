How to Play

  • Villagers run themselves. Place a building and free villagers build it, then fill its worker slots. Use − / + on a building to move workers around.
  • Haul & store. Workers carry resources to the nearest storage: Lumber Mills (wood), Mining Camps (stone/gold), Granaries (food), Storehouses, or the Town Hall. Full storage stops work, so build more.
  • Every villager is a person with a name, age, home and skills that improve with practice. They sleep at home at night, children come of age at 14, and elders slow down.
  • Seasons. Spring and summer grow, autumn gives bumper harvests, winter freezes the fields. In winter every occupied home burns firewood; cold villagers fall ill and can die.
  • Happiness comes from varied meals (wheat, bread, meat), ale, a warm home and safety. Happy towns work faster, have babies and attract settlers. Miserable people leave.
  • Production chains. Wheat → Windmill → flour → Bakery → bread. Wheat → Brewery → ale. Wood → Firewood Yard → firewood. Hunters bring back meat from the deer herds.
  • Planks & iron. A Sawmill turns wood into planks, which military and advanced buildings need. Mine iron ore (set a Mining Camp to Iron), then smelt 2 ore + 1 firewood into iron for swords, knights and upgrades.
  • Logistics. Drag roads (Q) for 50% faster travel. A Cart Shed's carters haul output piles from farms and workshops, 30 at a time. The Market buys and sells for gold, and its caravans earn gold at the Trading Posts on the map's edge (enemies can ambush them).
  • Your faction's heroes. Each Hollow Vale faction brings its own roster. The Barracks trains infantry, ranged troops, casters and squads (three bodies per training). The Stable trains cavalry and chargers, the Siege Workshop your siege heroes (plus rams, catapults and towers for everyone), and the Hero Shrine (Castle Age) summons Elites, one of each at a time.
  • Faction powers carry over. Kingdom Formation (less damage beside an ally), Vampire Bloodthirst (attacks heal), Undead Undying (rise once), Infernal Burn, Wilds Regrowth, Iron Clan Siegecraft (extra damage to buildings), Arcane Flow (faster attacks). Unit traits too: fliers cross walls and water, chargers hit harder after a run, splash, stun, drain and healing. Heroes earn stars from kills.
  • Walls. Drag Palisades (cheap wood) or Stone Walls along a line. Pick Gate and click one of your wall tiles to swap it for a gate: your people pass, enemies must break it.
  • Siege. A Siege Workshop builds Rams (smash buildings), Catapults (long-range splash, weak up close) and Siege Towers (park beside an enemy wall to let troops climb over).
  • Formations, stances, morale. Pick Line, Wedge or Spread and Aggressive, Defensive or Hold Ground in the army panel. Losing fights, being outnumbered or seeing an Elite fall drains morale; broken troops flee, then regroup.
  • Elites lead. An Elite's aura boosts nearby troops' attack, armor and morale, and grows with its stars.
  • The land. Hills slow marching troops, but archers on a hill shoot 1 tile further. Fords let you wade across rivers, slowly. The northern highlands have poorer harvests and colder winters but plenty of stone and iron. The dry southern plains farm well, have fewer trees and richer gold.
  • Points of interest. Send anyone to search Ancient Ruins for loot. Hold a Shrine with your units for 12 s to claim it (+6 happiness). Bandit Camps raid nearby farms; burn one down for its gold hoard.
  • Water. Build a Dock (Gather tab) on the shore. Fishing Boats bring in food all year. Transports carry 10 people: select units and right-click the boat to board, then select the boat and right-click a shore to land them. Warships (Town Age) fight at sea. On the Islands map every town starts on its own island.
  • Saving. Save and load from the pause menu (P). The game also autosaves every 2 minutes.
  • Ages. Advance from the Village Age to the Town Age and then the Castle Age at your Town Hall. Each age unlocks buildings and units, and lets you adopt one of two doctrines. That choice is permanent.
  • Rivals and diplomacy. Each rival has a ruler with a personality: Builders fortify, Warlords raid, Merchants trade. Open Diplomacy (or click a rival's dot) to offer peace, propose alliances, send tribute or declare war. Win by conquest, or when every rival still standing is your ally.
  • Town bell. Ring it from the Town Hall: villagers shelter in the Hall and towers, which shoot extra arrows for each person inside.
