Rig alignment corrections

Humanoid upper-arm and forearm cutouts now attach using opaque-band joint centers.
Each source bone is aligned from its shoulder/elbow root to the next joint;
diagonal artwork and mirrored pieces no longer inherit a false vertical axis.
Weapon grips stay at the hand joint. Attack wind-ups retain forward elbow flex.

Demon Prince wings use per-facing shoulder roots and matching screen-side flap
signs. The membrane fans outward and retains the source aspect ratio. Original
PNG source artwork is unchanged; build-batch.py preserves the corrected mapping.

Visual QA: down-right idle/action comparisons for Frost Witch, Pyromancer,
Knight, Brute, Gunner and Seer; Demon Prince all42 pose/facing combinations,
without edge clipping. Preview images are arm-alignment-review.png and
infernal/demonprince/runtime-poses.png.
