HOLLOW VALE CHARACTER ART AND EFFECTS REQUIREMENTS

Every new character art package includes the associated visual effects needed
by that character's existing abilities, alongside its movable body and equipment
pieces. Effects use the same Arcane Vanguard palette, shading and outline style.

For each character, audit its actual abilities and status effects first. Supply
applicable casting effects, projectiles, trails, impacts, ground effects, auras,
status overlays and spawn/death effects. Do not invent gameplay mechanics solely
to justify artwork. Shared effects may be reused if visually appropriate.

Frost Witch example: separate icicle projectile, snowflake particles, frost
impact, casting frost aura and a frozen-status overlay where the game supports
that status. Include projectile launch/travel/impact and aura start/loop/fade.

Keep effects separate from body atlases so they can move, rotate and animate
independently. Use transparent assets with explicit source rectangles, pivots,
attachment anchors, frame order, timing, looping and cleanup information.
Provide facing behavior for all six directions: directional frames or rotation
for symmetric projectiles, and appropriate layering behind/in front of the unit.

Record the gameplay event triggering each effect, its duration and termination
condition. Effects must respect visibility rules unless an ability explicitly
reveals targets. Preview idle, cast, impact and effect expiration; check that
effects do not persist after death, scene reset or interrupted animations.

Visual effects and audio are separate deliverables. Provide event hooks for
cast, launch, hit and expiration where appropriate; do not claim sound assets
exist unless they have actually been created and validated.

Each faction report must distinguish generated assets, preview validation,
game integration and remaining roster coverage. A representative character
package does not mean the entire faction is complete.
