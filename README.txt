HOLLOW VALE
===========
1v1, 3-player or 4-player battles, online or on one device.

GAME MODES (main menu)
- Play online: create a game, pick 1v1 / 3 players / 4 players in the lobby,
  and send friends the link or room code. Empty seats are played by the AI.
- Play local (this device): pick the number of players and set each seat to
  Human or AI. Humans take turns on the same device; a "pass the device"
  screen appears between turns. One human + AIs = solo play.
- Heroes: Choose (draft a 10-slot team) or Random (everyone is dealt a random
  10-slot team; a rematch deals new teams). Set it in the lobby or local setup.

BOARDS
- 1v1: small hexagon, Player 1 bottom vs Player 2 top.
- 3 players: big hexagon, every other edge (bottom, upper left, upper right).
- 4 players: square field, one player per side (bottom, left, top, right).

ALWAYS ON
- Terrain: forests (-1 damage from ranged attacks), hills (+1 range for
  ranged heroes), swamps (walking heroes stop when they enter). Random each
  game but mirrored so every seat gets the same ground.
- Crits & counters: attacks on heroes can crit for +50% damage (10%, +10%
  when flanking, +5% per star). A melee hero that survives a melee hit
  strikes back for half its damage times its HP% (rounded, min 1) unless
  stunned or silenced. Towers strike back at heroes that hit them from the
  next hex: 3 at full HP times the tower's HP% (min 1); Farms, Watchtowers,
  Barracks, Mana Wells, Cottages, the Great Hall and walls strike back for
  1. Ranged attacks from 2+ hexes are never struck back. Red target hexes
  preview the damage, crit chance, counter and "Tower strikes back for X".
- Movement: heroes walk their route hex by hex (about 0.12 s a hex, fliers
  glide in an arc). It is only animation: instant in replays and with
  reduced motion, and the AI never waits more than 0.6 s for a walk.
- Fog of war: you only see enemies within sight of your heroes, towers and
  buildings (most heroes 3 hexes, fliers and long-range 4, heavies 2, scouts
  5-6, watchtowers 4). Walking into a hidden enemy is an ambush. Each faction
  has a scout that fires flares to light up an area.
- Gold: +2 a turn, +2 per gold mine, +1 per farm, +1 interest per 10 saved
  (max 2). Also 1 per kill (2 for a boss), 2 per enemy building, 3 per tower,
  4 per treasure chest (one appears in the middle every 3 rounds).
- Market: Hire any unit from your main faction (3-8 gold, bosses 14, army of
  up to 20 slots, more with Cottages and a Great Hall; the draft is still
  10 slots). Recruit a fallen starting hero (5, bosses 8, +2 each time).
  Repair a tower by 4 (3, +1 each time). Upgrades: Forge Weapons, Forge
  Armor, Fortify Towers, Scout's Lens, Deep Shafts.
- Buildings (next to your heroes or towers, not near enemy towers): Farm
  (+1 gold a turn), Watchtower (sees 4), Barracks (spawn next to it), Mana
  Well (+1 mana a turn), Cottage (5 gold, +2 army slots, up to 3), Great Hall
  (12 gold, +5 army slots, 1). Max army cap is 31. Enemies can knock them
  down; if a Cottage or Great Hall falls your cap drops, your units stay, but
  you cannot hire or recruit until you are back under the cap.
- Towers never lose HP on their own before sudden death. From round 14 every
  tower crumbles each round (2 HP, +1 every 3 rounds) and can no longer be
  repaired or fortified.
- Splash hits enemies next to the target for half damage. Melee splash is a
  cleave: the 2 hexes beside the target, in front of the attacker. The
  Lancer's spear pierces 2 hexes in a straight line for full damage.
- Combos: Flank (+1 melee damage when an ally is also next to the target),
  Shatter (+2 damage to stunned heroes), Rally (+1 damage next to your boss).

MATCH OPTIONS (lobby host / local setup screen)
- Shrines (on): a hero on a shrine at the start of your turn gives +1 mana
  and heals 2.
- Poison mist (off): from round 8 a poison fog creeps in from the edges.
- Hero levels (on): 2 kills = a star (+2 max HP, +1 damage), 5 kills = 2 stars.
- Ban phase (on): every human player bans one hero before the draft.
- Spell cards (off): draw one a turn, hold 3. Fireball, Frost Bolt, Meteor,
  Mend, Haste, Stonemason, Tribute. Spells cost mana and ignore armor.
- Bounties (on, 3-4 players): the leader (most tower HP) wears a crown;
  destroying one of its towers gives +2 mana.
- Truces (off, 3-4 players): offer on your turn, they accept on theirs. No
  attacks between allies for 3 rounds. Betraying gives the victim +2 mana.
- King of the Hill (off): start 3 of your turns in a row holding the hill
  in the middle (no enemy on it) and you win.
- Boss Raid (off, co-op): everyone teams up against an AI raid boss in the
  last seat. It enrages at 2/3 HP and raises skeletons at 1/3. Kill it (or
  its towers) to win. It gets tougher with more players.
- Ultimates (off): deal 15 damage to fill your meter, then unleash your main
  faction's ultimate.

NEW HEROES
- Trapper (Wilds): hides snares (1 mana, up to 3, last 5 rounds). An enemy
  walking onto one stops and cannot move on its next turn. Flyers are immune.
- Stonewright (Iron Clan): builds 6 HP walls (2 mana, up to 3) that block
  movement and ranged shots. Enemies can knock them down.
- Hexweaver (Arcane Order): Silence (2 mana) on a 3-hex-wide area up to 3
  away. Enemies there cannot attack or use abilities on their next turn.

READING THE BOARD
- Icons above heroes: 🏇 charge, 🏹 ranged / ⚔️ melee, 🩸 lifesteal, 🔥 fire,
  💫 stun, 👁 scout, ⚰️ raise the slain. A grey hero with a ✓ has already
  acted this turn.
- Tap or click an enemy to see where it can move (blue) and hit (red).
- The 👁 button shades every hex an enemy can hit next turn.
- Hover (or tap) anything on the hero card for an explanation.

MOVING
- Select a hero, then move the mouse over the blue hexes: an arrow shows the
  exact path it will walk (it matters for snares). Click the arrow tip to lock
  the path (it turns green), click it again to move. Esc or right-click
  cancels. On a phone: tap a blue hex to draw the path, tap more blue hexes to
  bend it, tap the tip again to move.

CAMPAIGN (main menu)
- An illustrated map of the Vale with 15 battles and branching paths. Win a
  battle to open the paths beyond it and pick your own route. Crown nodes are
  boss fights; the skull keep is the final raid against the Dread Colossus.
  Progress is saved in the browser on that device.

BOSSES (5 team slots each, 5 per faction, each with a signature mechanic)
- Kingdom: Lionheart King (Royal Command), High Seraph (Holy Aura), Warlord
  Gideon (War Banner), Grand Templar (Tower Shield), Lord Marshal (Momentum)
- Vampires: Vampire Lord (Blood Feast), Blood Queen (Exsanguinate),
  Nosferatu (Mist Step), Crimson Matriarch (Blood Pact), Bat King (Swarm Call)
- Undead: Lich King (Raise Dead), Bone Colossus (Ossuary), Death Knight
  (Withering Strike), Banshee Queen (Wail), Plague Lord (Plague Aura)
- Infernal: Ember Dragon (Wings of Flame), Pit Lord (Immolation), Inferno
  Witch (Hellfire), Demon Prince (Hellgate), Phoenix (Rebirth)
- Wilds: Ancient Mammoth (Trample), Elder Treant (Entangle), Alpha Direwolf
  (Pack Howl), Storm Roc (Gale), Cave Bear (Thick Hide)
- Iron Clan: Stone Titan (Aftershock), War Engine (Ballistic Arc), Forge
  Master (Field Repairs), Iron Juggernaut (Spiked Plating), Thunder King
  (Chain Lightning)
- Arcane Order: Starweaver (Arcane Surge), Chronomancer (Time Slow), Void
  Sorcerer (Mana Burn), Mirror Mage (Reflection), Runekeeper (Rune Ward)
- Aura bosses show their area on the board (green gas, fire ring, holy glow,
  banners...). Hover a hex to see which aura covers it.
- Hovering a hero on PC shows its full card docked at the bottom of the screen.

REJOINING A GAME
- Lost connection, closed the tab or refreshed? Open the game link again, or
  open the site: the main menu shows a green "Rejoin game XXXX" button.
- Playing from a different phone? Open the room link; you join as a
  spectator and can tap "Take over <name>'s seat" for any player who is
  disconnected.


Two ways to host online games:

A) ONLINE (recommended for iPhones): put the game on a free website once, then
   everyone just opens the link. See "HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE.txt".

B) HOME WIFI: one computer runs the game while you play.
   1. Install Node.js (free LTS version) from https://nodejs.org
   2. Double-click "Start Hollow Vale (Windows).bat"  (Mac: right-click
      "Start Hollow Vale (Mac).command" > Open).
   3. Click "Play online" > "Create a new game". Friends on the same wifi
      scan the QR code or open the address shown. Keep the black window open.
   If Windows asks about the firewall, click "Allow access".

PC AND MOBILE VERSIONS
- The same link works everywhere. Phones and tablets get the mobile version
  (big buttons, zoomable board, reserve tray at the bottom, compact hero cards
  with an "i" button for details). Computers get the PC version (hover
  tooltips and keyboard shortcuts: 1-5 pick reserve, Tab next hero, Esc cancel,
  E end turn, right-click to deselect).
- To switch, use the link under the main menu buttons
  ("Switch to the PC/mobile version"). You can also add ?v=pc or ?v=mobile to
  the address.

MUSIC
- The main menu has its own theme. It starts after your first tap or click
  (browsers block autoplay) and fades out when a battle starts.
- Turn it off with the music button in the top-right corner. Your choice is
  remembered.

IN THE GAME
- Everyone types a name and taps "Sit here". Empty seats are played by the AI.
- The first person in the room is the host and starts the hero draft.
- Free-for-all. Turns go in seat order. From round 14 (sudden death) every
  tower crumbles at the start of each round. Last player standing wins.
- Cavalry units (Outrider, Boar Rider, Bone Rider, Blood Hunter) have Charge:
  they can move AND attack in the same turn, but are fragile and hit softly.
- Every faction has 5 bosses (5 team slots each) with attack cutscenes.
  The Lich King can also spend 2 mana to raise skeletons next to him
  (select him, tap "Raise skeleton", then a green hex).
- Necromancer (Raise the Slain): when its attack, counterattack or splash
  kills an enemy hero, a random Undead hero of the same gold cost (or the
  nearest cheaper one) rises on that hex or next to it and fights for the
  Necromancer's side. A slain boss raises an Undead boss. An enemy squad
  raises once, when its last member falls; a risen squad rises whole.
  Summoned and raised units never rise, and a raised Necromancer cannot
  raise. Raised units start used, show a countdown and crumble after 3 of
  their owner's turns. They have no Undying or on-death effects, do not
  count toward the army cap and cannot be recruited. No free hex means
  nothing rises.
- Towers have 12, 14 and 15 HP.
- Dropped out or refreshed? Open the link again: your seat and the battle come
  back. The host can also hand a missing player's seat to the AI.

IPHONE TIPS
- Add to Home Screen (Safari > Share) to get an app icon that opens full screen.
- Tap a hero in your reserve, then a glowing hex to spawn. Tap your unit, then a
  blue hex to move or a red hex to attack. Tap enemies or towers to see stats.
- Turn the phone sideways for a bigger board. No sound? Turn off silent mode.

SOLO
- Open public/index.html in a browser and choose "Solo: you vs 2 AI".
